Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 3, 2026 | Document Reference: POL-PRIV-01

1. Scope & Educational Nature

This Privacy Policy outlines how data is handled by the Campus Landmark A* Route Planner application. This tool is developed strictly for academic, research, and educational demonstration purposes within the Artificial Intelligence Laboratory curriculum.

2. Data Collection and Usage

We respect user privacy and adhere to data minimization principles:

  • No Personally Identifiable Information (PII): This application does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials.
  • No Tracking Cookies: The service does not deploy third-party advertising trackers, cross-site telemetry, or behavioural analytics scripts.
  • Local Query Processing: Route calculations, landmark lookups, and benchmark statistics execute in-memory on the host application instance.

3. Mapping Data and OpenStreetMap Attribution

Street topologies and tile imagery are retrieved from OpenStreetMap contributors under the Open Database License (ODbL). Standard HTTP headers (e.g., User-Agent) are transmitted to map tile endpoints strictly to load map graphics in accordance with OSM tile usage policies.

4. Local Caching

Geospatial network models (.graphml files) are cached locally in the filesystem to prevent redundant queries to public Overpass API endpoints and ensure consistent offline execution.

5. Contact Information

For questions or verification regarding this academic implementation, please refer to the project repository documentation at github.com/giricharan1603/a-planner.
